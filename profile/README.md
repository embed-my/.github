Apps that make and play H5P activities, with no H5P server.

**Embed My** builds apps on Missing Elements' open-source [H5P player and tools](https://github.com/missing-elements/h5p-toolkit): an activity is built and played in the person's browser, with a `.h5p` file for Moodle or any other H5P site, and nothing they make is kept by us.

## Apps

**EmbedMy Lesson**, an app for ChatGPT: describe a lesson and get an interactive H5P activity in the reply, from a quiz to a branching scenario, to play there and download. Its [support](https://embed-my.org/support/), [privacy](https://embed-my.org/privacy/) and [terms](https://embed-my.org/terms/) are on embed-my.org; the site's code and the app's server are in [embed-my/website](https://github.com/embed-my/website).

More at [embed-my.org](https://embed-my.org/).
